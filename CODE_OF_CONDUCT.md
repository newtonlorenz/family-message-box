# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming, practical, and safe for contributors of
all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus on the
work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to a
maintainer through their public GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
